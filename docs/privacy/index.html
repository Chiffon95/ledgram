Privacy Policy

English · 한국어

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Information stored on your device

LEDgram stores Editor preferences in app-local storage on your device. These preferences include message text; text and background colors; Bold, Italic, and Underline styles; Render Style; Motion and Effect selections; and Motion Speed and Effect Speed.

LEDgram also stores local Display-exit counters and interstitial timestamps used to control advertising frequency. LEDgram does not send this locally stored Editor content or frequency-control data to Gowl Labs.

Your content and app services

LEDgram does not upload your LED message text to Gowl Labs. The app has no user accounts and no Gowl Labs backend or server for user content.

LEDgram does not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics.

Advertising and consent

LEDgram uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertising in the Editor and uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent and provide privacy choices where applicable. Display mode itself contains no ads.

According to Google’s Mobile Ads SDK disclosure, the SDK automatically collects and shares certain information for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. This may include IP address, user interactions with the app, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers. Google processes this information under its own terms and policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure
  • Google UMP privacy documentation

Your controls

You can reset Editor settings locally from LEDgram’s Settings screen. Editor reset does not reset Google UMP consent or privacy choices, advertising state, or advertising-frequency counters and timestamps because those settings have separate purposes and controls.

When required, LEDgram provides a Privacy Options entry in Settings for choices managed through Google UMP. Android’s system settings may also provide controls for the device’s advertising identifier.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated if LEDgram’s behavior changes. The effective date above identifies this version of the policy.
